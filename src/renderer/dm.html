          Mark a rectangle on the map

          1. Drag corners 1–4 onto the corners of a block of grid squares, or any rectangle you know the size of.
          2. Enter its size in squares. One square becomes 1 inch on the table.
          3. Press “Straighten and place”. A slanted photo comes out square.

          Map

          Select (V) moves the map by whole inches, or a disc by its squares; Lock keeps the map in place, and then dragging pans. Pinch, the mouse wheel or + and − zoom; Fit shows it all. Reveal (R) and Hide (H) paint the fog; Marker (M) places discs. Two fingers cancel a stroke, and so does Esc. The dashed line is the edge of the table screen.

          Table screen

          The test grid shows 1-inch squares. The curtain (top right, or B) blanks the table while you set up. Disc letters read upright from the bottom edge of the picture; Flip turns them to face the far side.

          Calibration

          1. With a ruler, mark a rectangle on the mat: a coin or tape at each corner.
          2. Enter its size, then drag corners 1–4 below until each lands on its mark on the table.
          3. Check one square with the ruler. It should be 1 inch.

          Arrow keys move the selected corner 1 pixel on the table screen; hold Shift for 10.
