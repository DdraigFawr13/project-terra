Get the table ready

  1. Table screen
  2. Calibrate
  3. Import a mapA picture or photo of a map, or a VTT file (.dd2vtt, .uvtt) with its walls and doors. Maps stay in your library on this laptop.
  4. Add the party

    Mark a rectangle on the map

    1. Drag corners 1–4 onto the corners of a block of grid squares, or any rectangle you know the size of. A magnifier shows the corner while you drag.
    2. Enter its size in squares, and the feet a square stands for. One square becomes 1 inch on the table.
    3. Press “Straighten and place”. A slanted photo comes out square.
    4. A picture with no squares, such as a region map: press “Fill the table” instead. It is turned if that makes it bigger, never stretched.

    Paint out room numbers

    You keep the numbers on your map; the table gets this copy. Fill: swipe over a number. Clone: tap clean floor, then paint over the number with it.

    Walls

    For you only: the table never shows these. Rooms uncover in one tap; Look from here and line of sight will use the walls. Draw with the pen; a finger moves the map.

    Calibrate the table

    1. With a ruler, mark a rectangle on the mat: a coin or tape at each corner.
    2. Enter its size, then drag corners 1–4 below until each lands on its mark on the table.
    3. Check one square with the ruler. It should be 1 inch.

    Arrow keys move the selected corner 1 pixel on the table screen; hold Shift for 10.
